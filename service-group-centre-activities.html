0136 · Group / Centre Activities

Shared experiences.
Meaningful connections.

Enjoy activities with others, explore your interests and practise everyday skills in a welcoming group setting.

Take part in a way that feels right for you

Group activities offer opportunities to meet people, learn together and enjoy shared interests. We discuss your preferences, accessibility needs and support requirements to help identify suitable activities. Contact us to discuss current programs and locations.

Support may include

  • Creative activities such as art and crafts
  • Shared cooking and practical skills activities
  • Social games and group conversations
  • Recreation and interest-based sessions
  • Practising cooperation and communication
  • Exploring community outings with others

How we work with you

We explain the activity, setting, schedule and available support before you decide. You can ask about accessibility, group size and any activity costs so you know what to expect. Activities depend on current availability and individual suitability.
