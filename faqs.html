FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers, without the jargon.

Find quick answers about starting support, choosing services and working with our team.

How do I get started?

Contact our team by phone or email. We’ll arrange a conversation to understand your goals, current supports and what you are looking for.

Can I choose which supports I receive?

Yes. Your services should reflect your goals, preferences and available funding. We will discuss the options with you before support begins.

Can my family or support coordinator be involved?

Yes. With your permission, we can include family members, carers, nominees or support coordinators in planning and reviews.

What areas do you service?

We support participants across Melbourne and surrounding areas. Contact us to confirm availability in your suburb.

Can I change my support schedule?

We understand that needs and routines change. Speak with our team as early as possible and we will work with you to adjust your schedule where available.

How do you match participants and support workers?

We consider your goals, preferences, personality, communication style, interests and the skills required for your support.

What if I am unhappy with my support?

Please tell us. We welcome feedback and will listen carefully, respond respectfully and work with you to resolve the issue.

Do you provide emergency or crisis support?

Our services are not an emergency service. If there is immediate danger, call 000. For urgent mental health support, contact an appropriate crisis service.

Still have a question?

Our team is happy to help.
