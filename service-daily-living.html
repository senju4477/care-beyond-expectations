Everyday support for living together.

Practical assistance with daily tasks in a shared home, shaped around your routines, preferences and independence.

Feel supported in your shared home

Living with others involves both personal routines and shared responsibilities. We work with you to identify the assistance you need while respecting your privacy, choices and relationships with housemates.

Support may include

  • Developing morning and evening routines
  • Planning and preparing meals together
  • Sharing everyday household responsibilities
  • Building communication with housemates
  • Practising independent living skills
  • Adjusting to new shared living routines

How we work with you

We start with your goals and discuss how support will fit your living arrangements. Together, we agree on routines and review them as your needs change. The type and availability of support are agreed before services begin.
