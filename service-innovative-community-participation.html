Find new ways to connect and contribute.

Explore your strengths and interests through personalised opportunities that build confidence, skills and meaningful community connections.

Discover what participation means to you

You may want to try something new, become more confident around others or find a way to contribute to your community. We work alongside you to explore possibilities and turn your interests into manageable steps.

Support may include

  • Identifying strengths, interests and personal goals
  • Exploring volunteering and community projects
  • Trying new activities with practical encouragement
  • Building confidence in unfamiliar settings
  • Developing social and communication skills
  • Planning steps towards ongoing involvement

How we work with you

We listen to what you want to achieve, explore suitable opportunities and agree on a practical plan. We review your experience together and adapt the support as your confidence grows.
